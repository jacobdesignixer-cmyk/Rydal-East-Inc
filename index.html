PROSPECTIVE BUYER FAQ

Everything You Need to Know About Buying at Rydal East

Purchasing in a co-op means acquiring shares in the corporation (Rydal East Inc.) that owns the property. With your shares comes a Proprietary Lease granting you exclusive, permanent occupancy rights to your specific residence. This cooperative structure keeps operational costs significantly lower, allows collective tax savings, and ensures that all residents are vetted owners invested in community upkeep.

Per Rydal East bylaws, co-op shares are typically purchased with cash. While some institutional co-op specialty lenders exist, most sales proceed on a cash basis. This eliminates complex mortgage underwriting delays and maintains exceptionally low default risk for the entire community.

Every prospective shareholder submits an application packet including financial verification, background review, and references. Following submission, prospective buyers attend an interview with the Rydal East Board of Directors. This process protects property values and maintains a friendly, respectful living atmosphere.

Upon closing, the purchaser contributes a one-time capital contribution / improvement fee (approximately $7,000). The initial application fees are credited toward this amount upon approval. This contribution directly funds the community’s long-term capital reserve fund for roof, mechanical, elevator, and exterior brick maintenance—insulating owners against surprise special assessments.

To maintain acoustic privacy between residences, Rydal East bylaws require wall-to-wall carpeting (with quality sound-dampening padding) in primary living and bedroom spaces. Kitchen and bathroom areas may feature hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, or tile flooring. Residents consistently praise this rule for making the building exceptionally peaceful.

The monthly fee is truly all-inclusive. It covers your real estate / property taxes, electric usage, radiant heating, central air conditioning, domestic water, municipal sewer, trash collection, basic cable television, seasonal outdoor pool privileges, off-street parking, master building hazard insurance, full-time landscaping, and snow removal.

Rydal East is a cat-friendly cooperative. Residents are permitted up to two (2) indoor domestic cats. Dogs are not permitted in the community to maintain quiet acoustic standards throughout the corridors and grounds.
